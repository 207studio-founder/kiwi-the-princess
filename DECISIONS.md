# Final Kiwi assembly decisions

The user requested the original GPT artwork and anatomical Three.js workflow, with Codex performing the work directly. The procedural Blender R7 candidate changes Kiwi's face, ears and surface and is excluded. Existing painted side rigs evaluated in Blender and actually drawn by Three.js remain valid source artifacts; this is not adoption of the rejected procedural model.

User-authored depictions override generic hatch-pet defaults: greeting is held-out tongue plus tail wag; waiting is frontal prone sleep with breathing, Z marks and a bone dream; running is keyboard activity; review compares two bones. The approved native RGBA renderer replaces chroma generation and despill stages. The v2 layout, counts, timings, neutral cell, alpha checks, independent visual gate and three blind direction reviews are retained.

Each renderer family has one fixed anatomical anchor and scale for every animation frame, documented in selection.json. Front sit/greet use 520x590 and 0.235. Side travel uses 810x590 and 0.235. Sleep and four activities now share the canonical original face and 520x590 world, exported on a padded 640x680 canvas at 0.235. Their source anchor [320,610] maps the same anatomical [260,550] to cell [96,190]. The prior 661x793/0.205 family changed head and material and is excluded. No per-frame bounding-box resizing or recentering occurs. Generated gaze rows use the skill's shared-scale lower-body registration before assembly. Sparse alpha values <=4 in source renders are numerical edge residue and are cleared before clipping checks. Fully transparent pixels have zero RGB.

The compact sleeping body was redrawn after the user rejected splayed legs. Its two front paws rest together below the chin and the rear legs are tucked out of view. Original facial pixels are retained over the registered prone artwork, with the closed-eye inserts only. Complete neck underpainting prevents holes when the original rigid face moves. The rejected splayed render is retained as QA history and excluded from all distribution archives. Previous R3 sleep/rise experiments are neither active native inputs nor shipped renderers.

Installed packaging is a new kiwi-clay-r4 pet. Existing Kiwi, Mimi and other pets remain available. Artwork, source code, registration, raw captures and verification reports are retained for reproducibility and distribution; no broad temporary cleanup is performed.

The jumping state now depicts sit-pretty on two grounded hind paws, with two lifted forepaws: four paws total. The first six-paw generation was rejected; only the corrected hind-stand.png is selected. The canonical face and ears remain rigid. Standing upper-body motion leaves the ground band unchanged. Sleep uses one translucent breathing nose bubble; Zzz, dream clouds and dream bones are removed.

The review state is now sniffing followed by a curious head tilt. Its six frames lower the rigid head with a subtle expansion of the original nose pixels, recover, hold a slight tilt and return to neutral. The feet stay fixed, and bones are no longer loaded or drawn by the active renderer. No generated whole-face replacement or face-mesh deformation is added. The nose pulse is limited to scale1.028.

The head and ear cutouts share an eight-pixel support margin at their internal boundaries. Every added pixel comes from the untouched canonical artwork with its original RGB and alpha; external transparency is retained. Moving ears therefore reveal painted overlap beneath the rigid face instead of exposing the background through abutting masks. QA compares all 31 activity renders with the pre-correction baseline, requires that alpha never decreases, checks unchanged paw/lower-body bands and motion sources, and preserves all 43 unaffected native cells exactly.
